What it does

Three tools, one popup, zero servers in between.

Merge

Combine multiple PDFs into one, in whatever order you drag them into. Reorder, rotate, or drop individual pages before you export.

✂️

Split

Pull specific pages out of a PDF into a new file. Select exactly the pages you need — nothing else leaves the original document.

🗜️

Compress

Shrink oversized scans and photo-heavy PDFs by re-encoding embedded images, with a strength level you control.
